II. Adjutant's Assessment // for the recruiter's eyes

A wright turned commander. Across ten years he has built, scaled and held the war-cogitators of the Dominion through campaigns in the coin-vaults, the exchange-bastions, the colosseum data-vaults and the high news-spires — from real-time trading engines to a news-platform that ten-times-three-thousand souls touch each day.

Moving between such different theatres has taught him to make ready with grim speed, to choose the right rite for each war, and to swear fealty to no single tool or machine-spirit. He has raised greenfield works from bare rockcrete, hardened them against the warp of bad data, and grown cadres of wrights through review, mentorship and the trial of recruits — fighting shoulder to shoulder with the Enginseers (backend), the Watch (DevOps) and the Strategos (product).

He brings, besides, an artificer's eye for heraldry and form, the proofing-rites of Mocha and Jest, and a relentless hand at hunting faults in the cogitator's deepest cogs.
